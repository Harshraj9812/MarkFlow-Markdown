Kubernetes
deployment guide

A practical runbook for shipping services with confidence. From configuration to a healthy production deployment.

01   Before you begin

Make sure you have access to your cluster, a configured kubectl context, and an image in your registry.

ⓘ   NOTEAlways verify your current context before applying changes to a production cluster.

02   Define your deployment

Keep the manifest in version control alongside your service.

YAML / deployment.yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: api-service
spec:
  replicas: 3
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: api-service

03   Verify the rollout

Confirm that the deployment is healthy before moving on.

05   Reliability calculations

Inline math flows with your text: availability is A=1−E/R, where E is failed requests and R is total requests.

A=(1−ER)×100%

Use display equations for formulas that deserve their own space, such as the mean request latency:

L=1n∑i=1nti
View equation source
Inline: $A = 1 - E / R$

$$
A = \left(1 - \frac{E}{R}\right) \times 100\%
$$

$$
L = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}t_i
$$
